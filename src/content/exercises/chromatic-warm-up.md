## The exercise

1. Start on the low E string. Play frets 1, 2, 3, and 4 using your index, middle, ring, and little finger.
2. Alternate downstrokes and upstrokes, playing one note per click.
3. Repeat on each string, moving toward the high E.
4. Work back toward the low E, playing frets 4, 3, 2, and 1 on each string.

## Focus on

- Keep your hands relaxed and your fretting fingers close to the strings.
- Aim for an even sound and clean notes before increasing the tempo.
- Slow down whenever tension builds. Stop if anything hurts.
